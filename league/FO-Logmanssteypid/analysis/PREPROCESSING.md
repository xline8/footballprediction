# Step 2 — Preprocessing: FO-Logmanssteypid

## Inclusion rules

- Played matches only: all 6 index rows are finished matches
  (`header.status.finished: True`, `reason.long: Full-Time` except the
  three penalty finals, `Pen 3–4` / `Pen 3–5` / `Pen 3–4`); none
  excluded, none missing.
- Season labels are the warehouse `season` values `2021`…`2026`
  (calendar-year labels; the final is played Sep–Dec).
- No deduplication was needed: 6 index rows = 6 distinct match ids =
  6 payload files on disk.

## Parsing rules (from payload inspection, 6/6 read)

- Score: `header.teams[0/1].score`. There is no second count to
  cross-check against: `content.matchFacts.events.events` is an empty
  list in all 6 payloads (0 events total — no goal minutes, no card
  events, no substitutions).
- Team stats: `content.stats` is `None` in 6/6 payloads — recorded as
  missing, never imputed. There are no period splits at all.
- Shotmap: `content.shotmap.shots` is `[]` in 6/6 payloads (the key
  exists; the list is empty). No SOT source exists anywhere.
- Cards: no source exists — no Card events (events feed empty), no
  stats-block `yellow_cards`, no referee field (`matchFacts` has no
  referee key in any of the 6 payloads). Red-card counts exist only as
  header counters (`numberOfHomeRedCards` / `numberOfAwayRedCards`,
  0/0 in all 6).
- Final context: `general.leagueRoundName` / `matchRound` (`"final"`),
  `header.status.reason` (penalty shootout scores), `whoLostOnPenalties`.
  The 90-minute score is the match-level outcome; the penalty outcome
  is a tie-level result, recorded only as context (no model is built).
- Team-name spelling varies across years ("B36 Torshavn" vs
  "B36 Tórshavn"; "Vikingur" vs "Víkingur"); the CSV keeps the
  payload/index spelling per row, and the analysis normalises accents
  when counting distinct clubs (6, not 8).

## Split-source rules

- Period splits do not exist anywhere in this competition (no stats,
  no events). No 1H/2H share of any market can be computed, and no
  halves constants are frozen.
- No fabrication anywhere: absent stats/shotmap/events stay absent in
  the CSV (empty fields), and the builder script emits them as such.

## Output

`league/FO-Logmanssteypid/data/fo_logmanssteypid_matches.csv` — one row
per played match (6 rows), regenerated by `data/build_data.py` from
the warehouse index + payloads. The CSV is gitignored (repo norm);
the builder is committed. The CSV exists only as a record of the 6
finals and their payload-verified targets; it feeds no fitting,
because no fitting is done.
