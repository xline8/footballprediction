# Verdict — T-RO (RO-LigaI total shots)

**NOT BUILDABLE — no prereg beyond the design note, no fitting,
virgin NOT read.**

The candidate fails the template's feasibility condition: a valid
virgin window must contain the target. The raw `total_shots`
series changes definition exactly at the rich-feed boundary where
the virgin window begins (dev-era mean 17.8–18.7/match →
2025/2026: 26.1; 2026/2027: 29.4), while SOT, corners and goals
are continuous across the same boundary. In the rich era
total = SOT + off-target + blocked exactly (blocked ≈ 6.9/match —
the minimal-era feed evidently did not count blocked shots).

The total-minus-blocked reconstruction (virgin mean 19.2) was
considered and rejected as the unified target: its identity
cannot be verified on dev-era data (no components were stored
before 2025/2026) and the residual drift is unexplained
(+1.1/match in the virgin season, +4.7 by 2026/2027). Adopting it
silently would have rewritten the target on an assumption —
the same class of move the cards event-unification was allowed
only because events exist independently in EVERY era.

A future build can revisit total shots once a full season of the
rich feed exists inside a dev window. Nothing ships.
