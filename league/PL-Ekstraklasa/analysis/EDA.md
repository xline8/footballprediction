## Findings

**F1 — Stable format, changing levels.** All complete seasons have the same 18-team, 34-round, 306-match structure. The partial 2026/2027 season is higher in goals, SOT, corners, and shots, but is confirmatory only and is not read here.

**F2 — Target traits are repeatable.** Career-pooled split-half reliabilities are goals-for 0.603, goals-against 0.599; SOT-for 0.779, SOT-against 0.729; corners-for 0.646, corners-against 0.625; shots-for 0.823, shots-against 0.823; and yellows-for 0.370, yellows-against 0.227.

**F3 — Halves are presentation constants.** On 917 complete-split matches, the ratio-of-sums first-half shares are goals 0.4618, SOT 0.4584, corners 0.4723, shots 0.4592, and yellows 0.3338. True variance is approximately zero for goals (−0.0104 after sampling-noise subtraction), SOT 0.0005, corners 0.0001, and shots 0.0005; yellow true variance is 0.0056 and is retained as a descriptive constant, not a team feature.

**F4 — xG is era-bound.** Stats-xG reliability is 0.610 on the 2023/2024–2026/2027 era, but coverage is only 1.3%, 2.0%, 100.0%, and 100.0% across those seasons. Set-play share reliability is 0.482 on the same sparse-era measurement. Shotmap xG/shot reliability is 0.102 on its usable 2025/2026–2026/2027 era. Stats xG/shot reliability is 0.184.

**F5 — xGOT does not pass.** Era-guarded xGOT/shot reliability is −0.016. The usable xGOT era is 2025/2026–2026/2027; earlier populated shots are not comparable because the field is selectively present on on-target shots.

**F6 — Territory is strong.** Box-touch reliability is 0.808 for and 0.764 against; possession is 0.894; big chances are 0.705. These values support testing the reference territorial arms, subject to the feature verdicts.

**F7 — Referee signal is moderate.** Referee yellow-rate reliability is 0.501 across 42 referees with 20 or more usable matches in the split check. Coverage is 72.9% in 2021/2022, 100.0% in 2022/2023–2024/2025, 86.6% in 2025/2026, and 100.0% in 2026/2027.

**F8 — Newcomers are below the league mean in attack.** The 15 observed transitions contain three newcomers each. Newcomers average 1.203 GF and 1.633 GA per match versus pooled league team means of 1.345 and 1.345: gaps −0.142 GF and +0.288 GA. Transition GF/GA are 2022/2023 1.079/1.471, 2023/2024 1.108/1.755, 2024/2025 1.382/1.617, 2025/2026 1.088/1.608, and 2026/2027 1.357/1.714.

**F9 — Count-line anchors.** The dev period is 2021/2022–2024/2025 (n=1,224 including the 2021/2022 training seed; scored walk-forward seasons are 2022/2023–2024/2025, n=918). Line over-rates are computed over the full dev period, the BE convention. The nearest .5 lines are goals 2.5 at 0.482 over, SOT 8.5 at 0.492, corners 9.5 at 0.551, yellows 3.5 at 0.567, and total shots 25.5 at 0.526. Virgin is 2025/2026, n=306; 2026/2027 n=124 is reserved and not read.
