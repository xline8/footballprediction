Referee strictness is not adopted: split-half referee-card reliabilities are .343, .354, .470, .494 for 2021/22–2024/25, but only one referee has four or more named matches in 2025/26. Coverage is only 18/198 in the virgin. Box-touch and big-chance reliabilities are not measurable because those keys are absent.

The pooled pre-virgin (2021/22–2024/25, the fit window) half constants are 0.4471 for goals and 0.3644 for event-unified cards (coordinator-recomputed from hu_nbi.csv; the virgin season is excluded). By season, observed per-match share variance divided by its binomial noise floor is approximately 0.96/0.97/0.97/0.89/0.95 for goals and 0.98/0.92/1.03/1.09/0.95 for cards (2021/22 through 2025/26). This is consistent with a frozen league constant; the constants are presentation-only, not team features. SOT, corners, and total shots have no half source.

`eda_summary.json` contains the machine-readable values, and `charts/` is generated by `make_charts.py`.
