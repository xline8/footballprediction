# Phase 3 — EDA: Greece Super League 1 (2021/2022–2026/2027)

All figures are generated by `eda_compute.py`, stored in
`eda_numbers.json` / `inventory_summary.json`, and were cross-checked
by the coordinator against an independent recomputation from the raw
warehouse JSONs (coverage, levels, cards check: exact agreement).

Pooled complete seasons (n=1,192): home goals 1.396, away 1.147,
2.543 per match; home wins 42.0%, draws 26.2%, away wins 31.8%.
Levels against references: goals 2.54 is below PL (2.67) and J1
(~2.7); corners 8.79 is the lowest of the three (PL 9.98, J1 ~9.5);
yellows 4.71 is the highest (PL 4.03) — Greece is a low-corner,
high-card league. Phase goal levels (complete seasons): regular
2.524, championship 2.702, conference 2.958 (n=24), relegation 2.465.

**F1 — Format stable in size, changed in shape.** 14 teams in every
season; the play-off structure changed in 2024/2025 (see
DATA_UNDERSTANDING §1). Levels swing season to season (goals 2.35 in
2022/2023 vs 2.97 in 2023/2024) more than phases differ.

**F2 — Target traits are the most reliable in the programme so
far.** Career-pooled split-half reliabilities (19 teams): goals-for
0.867 / against 0.742; SOT 0.850 / 0.808; corners 0.833 / 0.714;
shots 0.907 / 0.880; yellows 0.686 / 0.488. Every count trait beats
its PL counterpart (PL best: shots 0.823) — a stable 14-team league
with ~90 matches per team in the corpus.

**F3 — Halves are presentation constants.** On the complete-splits
era 2023/2024–2025/2026 (n=707), ratio-of-sums first-half shares:
goals 0.4528, SOT 0.4530, corners 0.4862, shots 0.4611, yellows
0.3522. True variance after noise subtraction: goals ≤0, shots ~0,
SOT 0.0019; corners 0.0053 (true SD 7.3pp — the largest in the
programme, still a constant by programme rule: halves are never
modelled) and yellows 0.0048. Frozen constants in config.yaml.

**F4 — xG is era-bound to the virgin.** Stats xG is full only from
2025/2026; before that there are 12 + 6 stray matches. Its split-half
reliability (0.830, 14 teams) is therefore measured almost entirely
inside the virgin era and cannot support any dev arm. Gate Zero's X1
blend runs effectively goals-only through dev and the virgin fit
(the PL situation). Set-play xG share reliability is 0.193 and
set-play xG rate reliability 0.165 — both fail on reliability as
well as era.

**F5 — Shotmap ratios are reliable but unusable.** Shotmap xG/shot
reliability is 0.665 and stats xG/shot 0.670, but the shotmap era is
2025/2026–2026/2027 only: an era starting inside the virgin kills
the arm (ES lesson). xGOT/shot reliability is 0.264 (below the ~0.35
arm standard), era-guarded to 2025+ (population rate 99.7% there;
35.8% / 98.6% on the handful of stray 2023/2024–2024/2025 shotmaps).

**F6 — Territory is exceptionally strong.** Box-touch reliability
0.953 for / 0.855 against; possession 0.931; big chances 0.860.
Box-touch coverage: 0.0% in 2021/2022, 82.5% in 2022/2023, 89.6% in
2023/2024, ~100% from 2024/2025 — the min-8 trailing rule governs
the X7 arm's early-dev activity.

**F7 — Referee signal is weak; X8 fails.** 117 distinct referees
are named (Greece appoints many foreign referees for big matches);
only 15 meet the split-half sample threshold, and their yellow-rate
reliability is r = 0.262 — below every adopted referee trait in the
programme (BE 0.378, PL 0.501, J1 0.646). A coordinator re-split
with a random shuffle gave r = −0.07: the trait is not there.
Coverage is also uneven (0.0% in the 2021/2022 seed, 73.7% in
2024/2025). X8 is dropped; K-GR is Gate Zero only.

**F8 — Newcomers are weaker, thinly counted.** 9 newcomers over 5
transitions (1–2 per transition): GF 1.119 vs league team mean
1.276 (gap −0.157), GA 1.559 (gap +0.283). Same sign pattern as PL
(−0.142 / +0.288) on far fewer movers. X2 measured, not consumed;
SHRINK is the thin-data arm.

**F9 — Count-line anchors.** Dev period 2021/2022–2024/2025 (n=956
including the seed; scored seasons n=716). Over-rates (full dev):
goals 2.5 → 0.482; SOT 8.5 → 0.447 (7.5 → 0.562); corners 8.5 →
0.517 (9.5 → 0.386); yellows 4.5 → 0.520 (3.5 → 0.699). Shots use
the scored dev seasons because the seed season's shots feed is a
different measurement: 22.5 → 0.534 (23.5 → 0.447). Primary lines:
goals OU2.5, SOT 8.5, corners 8.5, yellows 4.5, shots 22.5.
On-target rate reliability is 0.083 — noise, as everywhere.
