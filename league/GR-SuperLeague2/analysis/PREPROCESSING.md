# Phase 2 — Preprocessing: GR-SuperLeague2

Builder: `league/GR-SuperLeague2/data/build_data.py` (adapted from
the GR-SuperLeague builder; league_id 8815, scores-only, no stats
columns). Outputs (gitignored, regenerated by the builder):
`gr2_matches.csv` (1,935 rows, one per played match) and
`gr2_team_matches.csv` (3,870 rows).

Inclusion and provenance rules:

1. Played matches only — every index row for league_id 8815 is a
   played match with a score; all 1,935 are kept, ids unique
   (asserted).
2. Phase from `general.leagueName` only: North/South/Grp. A/Grp. B/
   bare `Super League 2` → `regular`; `Grp. A/B Promotion Group` →
   `playoff_promotion`; `Grp. A/B Relegation Group` →
   `playoff_relegation`; `Super League 2 Playoff` → `playoff`;
   `Super League 2 Super Cup` → `supercup`; any other value raises.
   Per-season phase counts are asserted against the format arithmetic
   (544 + 2 in 2021/2022; 420 in 2022/2023; 264 + 40 + 60 in
   2023/2024; 180 + 40 + 40 + 1 in 2024/2025; 180 + 24 + 60 + 1 in
   2025/2026; 79 regular so far in 2026/2027).
3. Scores-only assertion: the builder asserts `content.stats is
   None` and the shotmap is empty for every payload, and raises if
   any payload ever carries stats — so a future re-pull that added
   stats would surface loudly instead of silently changing the
   corpus. No stats columns are emitted at all.
4. HT goals come from goal-event times (`time <= 45`) and are
   carried only where the event feed exists; `ht_validated` is 1
   only where the event FT goal count reproduces the FT score (1,730
   of 1,736 event matches; the 6 mismatches are flagged, not
   repaired). Matches without an event feed carry `ht_source =
   none` and null HT goals — never inferred, not even for 0–0s.
5. B-team flags (`is_b_home` / `is_b_away`) are name-based (name
   ends with ` B`); B teams are kept as ordinary rows (see
   DATA_UNDERSTANDING §2). Team identity everywhere is the FotMob
   team id, which absorbs the three same-id renames.
6. Event Yellow / YellowRed / Red counts are carried in their own
   columns as descriptive feed data; there is no unified cards
   target and no cards candidate.
7. Season labels are the warehouse labels (`2021/2022` …
   `2026/2027`); match order is (date, match_id).
8. The 35 awarded 3–0 / 0–3 results without an event feed are kept
   as ordinary rows with their FT scores; only their HT split is
   missing.
