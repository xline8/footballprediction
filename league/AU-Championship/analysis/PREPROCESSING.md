# Step 2 — Preprocessing: AU-Championship

## Inclusion rules

- **Scope = the true Australian Championship only.** A match is
  included iff payload `general.leagueName` starts with
  "Australian Championship" (Grp. A–D + Final Stage). 87 matches.
  The 1,246 state-league rows sharing parent id 8938 are excluded
  (see DATA_UNDERSTANDING.md §3): separate competitions, disjoint
  team sets, ~50% of scores unrecorded.
- **Played matches only:** all 87 Championship matches are
  `header.status.finished = True`; none excluded, none missing.
  (The 5 postponed/abandoned matches in the parent are all state
  fixtures and are excluded with the state leagues.)
- **Season labels** are the warehouse `season` values `2025` and
  `2026` (calendar-year labels; the competition runs Oct–Dec).
  2025 is complete (55); 2026 is TRANSITIONAL (32, rounds 1–4 of 6).
- **No deduplication was needed:** 87 index rows = 87 distinct match
  ids = 87 payload files on disk; no duplicate fixtures.

## Parsing rules (from payload inspection, 1,333/1,333 read)

- **Score:** `header.teams[0/1].score`, cross-checked against the
  index `score` column — exact match in 87/87 Championship matches.
  The stored score is the final score; for the one extra-time match
  (2025 QF APIA 1-1 Marconi, ET + pens) it may include ET goals —
  recorded as-is in the CSV (`extra_time = 1`, `shootout = 1`) and
  never repaired.
- **Phase / group / round:** from `general.leagueName`
  (`Australian Championship Grp. A` → phase `group`, group `A`;
  `Australian Championship Final Stage` → phase `final`) and
  `general.leagueRoundName` (`1`–`6` in groups; `1/4`, `1/2`,
  `final` in the final stage).
- **Shootout:** `header.status.whoLostOnPenalties` non-null.
  Format change 2025 → 2026 (see DATA_UNDERSTANDING.md §4): 2026
  group draws go to a shootout with no ET (8/8); 2025 group draws
  do not (0/12). The stored score is the drawn score in both
  seasons; the shootout decides a bonus point, not the 1X2 result.
  The CSV records the flag only — no points table is built, because
  no model is built.
- **Targets that do not exist:** `content.stats` is `null` in all
  87 Championship payloads, `content.shotmap.shots` is `[]`,
  `content.matchFacts.events.events` is `[]`, the referee field is
  blank. SOT, corners, cards, total shots, xG, and period splits are
  recorded as missing, never imputed, never proxied (the red-card
  fields are near-zero-filled and are not used as a target).

## Split-source rules

- Period splits exist nowhere in this league (`content.stats` is
  null in 87/87). There are no 1H/2H constants and no halves
  presentation: every market would be FT-only, and no market is
  built.
- No trailing team statistic is constructed: with 6–9 matches per
  team per season there is no trailing window worth the name, and
  no candidate reaches design (see FEATURES.md / MODEL_DESIGN.md).

## Output

`league/AU-Championship/data/au_championship_matches.csv` (87 rows),
regenerated by `league/AU-Championship/data/build_data.py`
(asserts: parent rows = 1,333; state rows skipped = 1,246;
Championship rows = 87; every score cross-checked). The CSV is
gitignored by repo norm; the builder script is the committable
artifact (to be committed by the coordinator after the git rebuild).
