# Exploratory data analysis

All numbers below are generated by `run_eda.py` and stored in `eda_numbers.json`.

## Findings

**F1 — Inventory and levels.** Regular match counts are 112 in each season. Goals/SOT/corners/total shots are respectively 2.545/8.527/9.429/23.268 (2021), 2.500/7.571/9.125/21.446 (2022), 2.607/7.946/9.241/23.161 (2023), 2.545/8.036/9.580/23.455 (2024), and 3.000/8.393/8.768/23.679 (2025). 2026 is shown only as an in-progress inventory row.

**F2 — Trait reliability.** The 2023–2024 split-half correlations are goals-for 0.012, SOT-for 0.541, corners-for 0.715, total-shots-for 0.592, possession-for 0.873 and big-chance-for 0.127. Cards are assessed only in the credible era: yellows-for is 0.196 for 2023–2024 and 0.290 for 2024–2025; yellows-against is 0.463 and 0.531.

**F3 — Halves.** Frozen constants from 2023–2024 are goals 0.426343, SOT 0.435196, corners 0.466319, total shots 0.447807 and stats yellows 0.314532 for the first-half share. Observed variance versus binomial/Poisson noise leaves true SDs of 0.056009, 0, 0.018009, 0 and 0.040705 respectively; per-team half-share traits are not adopted.

**F4 — Referees and territory.** Named-referee coverage collapses to 40/117 all matches in 2025 after 110/117 in 2024. The available referee split-half strictness correlation is 0.371 across 16 referees with at least eight matches, but the virgin coverage collapse makes X8 unusable. Box-touch availability is 34/115, 28/117, 22/117, 117/117 and 117/117 in 2021–2025; X7 cannot meet an eight-match trailing requirement across most of the dev era. Possession is reliable; big chance is present in all payloads but its 2023–2024 for-trait reliability is only 0.127.

**F5 — Expansion entry.** Vancouver FC's 2023 first season was 1.000 GF and 1.786 GA per match versus a league team-match mean of 1.304: deviations −0.304 GF and +0.482 GA. This is one descriptive expansion instance, not a tier-mover estimate.

**F6 — Cards artefact.** Stats yellows average 1.152 and 0.938 in 2021/22, versus 4.348, 4.991 and 4.714 in 2023–2025. In 2025 stats and event-yellow totals agree exactly in 90/112 regular matches (means 4.714 and 4.938). The target is stats yellows, warm-up 2023, dev 2024 and virgin 2025; 2021/22 is not interpreted as a low-card era.

**F7 — Primary lines.** Dev over-rates closest to 0.5 select G-CA OU2.5 (0.496), S-CA 7.5 (0.547), C-CA 9.5 (0.487), K-CA 4.5 (0.549; 2023–2024 only) and T-CA 22.5 (0.507). The complete rates are in `eda_numbers.json`.
