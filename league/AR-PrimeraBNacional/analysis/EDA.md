CSV and stored in `eda_summary.json`. 2026 is strictly excluded from every
number. Reliability means reproduction on disjoint halves of the same teams'
matches: career-pooled teams with at least 24 matches, 60 random splits
averaged; ratio traits use ratios of sums. The halves noise floor is
`mean(p(1-p)/n_i)`.

## F3 — Halves

The frozen constants use complete-feed seasons only: goals first-half share is
**0.435381**, from 1,676 matches; yellow first-half share is **0.390091**,
from 1,954 group matches with at least one yellow. Yellow observed variance is
0.071372, with a 0.064181 per-match noise floor and +0.007191 true variance;
its team-level split-half r is **0.094**. Goal observed variance is 0.140347,
with a 0.143442 floor and −0.003095 true variance; its team-level split-half
r is −0.089.
The 2021–2022 goals-only-with-events check is reported separately: 1,060
matches, share 0.441417, true variance −0.000538. It is not pooled into the
frozen constant. The finding supports frozen presentation constants, not a
team half-tendency model.

## F8 — Referees

Named-referee coverage in the EDA seasons is 0.0%, 88.6%, 94.8%, 94.9%, and
97.2% for 2021–2025. Restricting strictness to the 2023+ yellow era and
referees with at least 8 matches gives 47 referees, split-half r **0.525**,

arm, with missing referee assignments falling back to the base arm.
