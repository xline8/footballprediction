# Verdict — G-AUCH (AU-Championship goals) — **NOT BUILDABLE**

No preregistration of arms was written and nothing was fitted. Scores
exist in 87/87 payloads (270 goals, mean 3.10/match; 2025 complete
n=55, mean 3.22; 2026 transitional n=32, mean 2.91), but the
competition has exactly one complete season: 2025 can only be the
training window (the analogue of J1's 2021), and season-blocked
walk-forward has zero evaluation seasons. There is no prior season
(the competition launched in October 2025), so there is no
prior-season climatology either. Per-team matches are 6–9 in 2025
(finalists 9), so Dixon-Coles ratings (32 parameters on 55 matches)
are not estimable to gate standard. The virgin window (2026, n=32)
has a calibration SE at OU 2.5 of ≈8.6pp — ~9× the 1pp clean-PASS
threshold — and one goal swings the over-rate by 3.1pp; a PASS/FAIL
there would be decided by one or two goals. Pooled n=87 still gives
SE ≈ 5.3pp. No X1 (no xG in any payload) and no X2 (zero club
overlap with AU-ALeague; state-league form is same-season concurrent
with 48% of scores unrecorded, and unvalidatable) can rescue the
candidate, and a round-blocked within-season dev would be a
program-frame change, not a league decision. The virgin was
**deliberately not read for evaluation** (2026 tallied at inventory
level only, to establish it is transitional). G-AUCH is NOT BUILDABLE.
Revisit: earliest dev = 2025+2026 with virgin = 2027, under a new
prereg; nothing is scheduled.
