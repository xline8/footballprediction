# Phase 3 — EDA: Greece Super League 2 (2021/2022–2026/2027)

All figures are generated by `eda_compute.py`, stored in
`eda_numbers.json` / `inventory_summary.json`. Scores-only league:
every figure below is goals-only unless it says otherwise; there is
no SOT / corners / shots / cards / xG / referee figure to report,
because none of those exist in any payload.

Pooled complete seasons (n=1,856): home goals 1.335, away 0.969,
2.303 per match; home wins 44.8%, draws 25.6%, away wins 29.6%;
OU2.5 over-rate 0.418. Levels against references: goals 2.30 is
the lowest in the programme so far (GR Super League 1: 2.54, PL:
2.67, J1: ~2.7) — a low-scoring second tier with a strong home
edge (home 1.335 vs away 0.969, the widest home/away split in the
programme). Levels are stable season to season (2.26–2.39), unlike
GR's 2.35–2.97 swings.

**F1 — Format shrank twice, then broke.** 34 teams in 2021/2022 →
30 → 24 → 20 → 20, then a single 16-team group in 2026/2027.
Churn is extreme: 33 newcomers across the 5 transitions (6–7 per
season), against GR's 9 across 5 transitions. The 2026/2027
single-group format means the confirmatory window is not the same
competition shape as the dev/virgin seasons — noted, not adjusted.

**F2 — Goals traits are reliable, on a thinner base than GR.**
Career-pooled split-half reliabilities (55 teams with ≥20 matches):
goals-for 0.656 / against 0.710. Real signal, below GR (0.867 /
0.742, 19 teams, ~90 matches each) — expected: SL2 teams average
~34 matches in the corpus and the league turns over a third of its
teams every season.

**F3 — Halves are a presentation constant (goals only).** On the
validated event subset of the complete seasons (n=1,617 matches
with goals), the ratio-of-sums first-half share is 0.4596, with
observed variance 0.1300 against binomial noise 0.1325 — true
variance −0.0025, true SD 0.0. Per-season shares: 0.472, 0.432,
0.472, 0.464, 0.455 (2026/2027 partial: 0.362 on n=72, a small-sample
wobble in an unfinished season, excluded from the constant). The
frozen goals constant is **0.4596** — slightly above GR's 0.4528
and J1's 0.412. No other market has a halves constant: there are no
splits for anything else.

**F4 — The tier effect is large, symmetric, and split by origin.**
Newcomers pooled (n=33): GF gap −0.074, GA gap +0.012 vs the league
team mean — a near-zero pooled gap that hides two opposite-signed
groups. Split by origin (previous-season tier, from the SL1
warehouse itself, FotMob 135):

| Newcomer origin | n | GF gap | GA gap |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| SL1-relegated | 8 (5 in dev) | **+0.314** | **−0.288** |
| Gamma Ethniki / other | 25 | −0.198 | +0.108 |

Relegated top-tier clubs arrive much stronger than the SL2 average
(dev-only: +0.384 / −0.326); promoted third-tier clubs arrive
weaker (−0.198 / +0.108). The SL1-relegated gap mirrors GR's
promoted-to-SL1 gap (−0.157 / +0.283) in the opposite direction and
is twice as large. See FEATURES X2 for why this is measured, not
consumed: 5 dev movers cannot carry an estimated offset, and a
single pooled newcomer offset would be wrong-signed for both
groups.

**F5 — B teams are league-average, open-playing sides.**
B-team matches: 528 (27.3%). B teams score 1.222 / concede 1.231
per match (gaps +0.070 / +0.079 vs the league team mean of 1.152);
B-involved matches average 2.455 goals vs 2.249 without a B team;
home-win rate is identical with/without a B team (44.9% vs 45.0%).
B teams are not pushovers and not defensive shells — they are
mid-table sides whose games run ~0.2 goals hotter. Keeping them as
ordinary rows (frozen rule) is supported, not merely convenient.

**F6 — Phase goal levels.** Pooled complete seasons: regular 2.297
(n=1,588), promotion group 2.635 (n=104), relegation group 2.188
(n=160), playoff final 1.000 (n=2), Super Cup 1.000 (n=2). The
promotion-group mean is driven by single seasons (3.05 in
2023/2024, 1.83 in 2025/2026) — noisy, and no phase feature exists
by rule.

**F7 — Event feed quality.** 1,736/1,935 matches carry events
(89.7%, rising from 84.5% to 94.3%); 1,730 validate exactly against
the FT score. The 6 mismatches and the 35 awarded 3–0/0–3
no-events results are documented in DATA_UNDERSTANDING §4. Event
cards are too sparse to support any target (337 Card events in
1,935 matches) and there is no stats feed to unify them against.

**F8 — Count-line anchor.** Dev period 2021/2022–2024/2025
(n=1,591; scored seasons n=1,045): OU2.5 over-rate 0.422 full-dev,
0.427 scored-dev. Primary line: goals OU2.5. (No other market has a
line: no target exists.)
