# EDA — NZ-Championship

All figures below are generated by `analysis/eda.py`; headline values
are mirrored in `eda_summary.json`. EDA includes 2025 and 2026 for
design-level inspection only. It does not fit, select, or gate an arm.

## F1 — Levels and home advantage

Goals per match for 2022–2026 are 3.9211, 4.1304, 3.8043, 3.4107 and
3.6571 (n = 38, 46, 46, 56, 35; 90-minute scores). Home/away goals per
match are respectively 2.1316/1.7895, 2.1087/2.0217, 1.9565/1.8478,
1.9643/1.4464 and 1.9714/1.6857. Home-win/draw/away-win rates are
0.5000/0.1842/0.3158, 0.3913/0.1522/0.4565, 0.3696/0.1957/0.4348,
0.5357/0.1429/0.3214 and 0.4286/0.2000/0.3714. Pooled rates are
0.4480/0.1719/0.3801. OU2.5 over-rates are 0.6842, 0.7391, 0.7609,
0.7500 and 0.6286; BTTS rates are 0.6842, 0.6957, 0.6522, 0.5893 and
0.5714.

This is a high-scoring league by any reference point (top-5 Europe
sits near 2.7 goals/match; IS-1Deild 3.2–3.6): even the lowest season
(2025, 3.41) is well above that. Home advantage is weak and
inconsistent — away wins outnumber home wins in both 2023 and 2024 —
so a fitted home advantage has little to lean on. The draw rate is
low (0.14–0.20 outside the 38-match 2022 season).

The dev OU2.5 over-rate is frozen at 0.75 pooled across 2023–2024
(69/92; by season 0.7391 and 0.7609). Phase levels are 3.7880
goals/match for 217 regular matches and 2.7500 for the 4 finals
(small n; finals are lower-scoring descriptively, not a modelled
effect).

## F2 — Reliability

Deterministic chronological odd/even split-half reliability, retaining
teams with at least 20 matches, gives goals-for r=0.2293 and
goals-against r=0.2570 across 10 teams. That is WEAK: team attacking
and defensive traits barely replicate across halves of a team's own
record in this league (IS-1Deild measured 0.80/0.82 on the same
protocol). A ratings model fitted on 38–92 matches therefore has
little stable team signal to find — the dev gate will decide whether
anything survives at all.

By season (≥8-match threshold, 5v5 split halves — extremely noisy):
GF/GA r is 2022 0.8056/0.4622 (6 teams), 2023 0.5294/0.2280 (10),
2024 0.1643/0.1059 (10), 2025 −0.0301/0.0839 (11); 2026 is not
computable (no team has 8 matches yet). SOT, corners, total shots and
cards are not testable: no target data exist.

## F3 — Halves

Among 178 event-backed matches with at least one full-time goal
(2023–2026; 2022 has no events), the ratio-of-sums constant is 282
first-half goals / 684 full-time goals = 0.4123. Season shares are
0.3684, 0.4057, 0.4503 and 0.4297. Observed per-match share variance
is 0.084888 versus a mean binomial noise floor of 0.086947 (excess
−0.002059: the observed variance is BELOW the noise floor).
Team-level odd/even split-half r is 0.0159 across 6 teams. This is
noise-level evidence, exactly as in J1 and IS-1Deild; the frozen
0.4123 constant is supported as a presentation-only split and halves
are not modelled. Per-season shares range more widely than J1's
(0.37–0.45 vs 0.41–0.46) because each season has only 45–56 matches
and ~130–190 goals — the season shares are consistent with binomial
noise around 0.4123 at those counts.

## F4 — Entrants

Entrant deviations are descriptive, computed against that season's
league mean per team per match. 2022 has no prior season, so its 10
rows are the baseline, not entrants. True entrants (no
previous-season record in league 8870): 2023 Eastern Suburbs
GF +0.4904 / GA −0.8430, Petone −1.3985 / +0.3792, Manurewa −0.2874 /
+0.1570; 2024 Birkenhead United (returnee) +0.4978 / −0.2022, Western

Spirit +0.0978 / +0.3200; 2025 Miramar Rangers (returnee) +0.7946 /
−0.3054, Christchurch United (returnee) −0.1054 / +0.1946, Auckland
United FC (returnee) −0.6054 / +0.1946, Auckland FC B −0.0054 /
−0.7054; 2026 (partial) Eastern Suburbs AFC, Napier City Rovers,
Cashmere Technical (all returnees) and Ferrymead Bays (debut). The
full club-level table is in `eda_summary.json`.

No origin can be assigned to any entrant: the clubs qualify from New
Zealand's regional leagues, none of which is in the warehouse, so
there is no mover prior to estimate and X2 is dropped. Note the
returnee pattern — most "entrants" are clubs returning after a
one-season absence, not first-timers — and the deviations are single-
season, ~9-match noisy means, not a usable signal.
