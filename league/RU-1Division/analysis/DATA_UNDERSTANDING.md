All counts and coverage below are generated by `eda_compute.py` and emitted
to `inventory_summary.json` / `eda_numbers.json`.

## Inventory and format

The build contains 1,756 unique matches and 3,512 team-perspective rows.
The season format is one regular phase with double round-robin arithmetic:
380 matches means 20 teams and 306 means 18 teams. The available 2025/2026
season has 305 matches, with one fixture missing. The in-progress 2026/2027
season has 153 matches. Every row has `phase=regular`.

The full pairing audit is in `inventory_summary.json`. Every complete season
has two meetings per unordered pair. In 2025/2026, 152 pairings were played
twice and one was played once: Ufa hosted SKA-Khabarovsk; the missing fixture
is the return meeting, SKA-Khabarovsk hosting Ufa. The JSON retains every pair and its home/away
directions.

## Payload facts

Events are present in 100.0% of matches. Stats coverage by key is recorded per
season in the JSON for `BallPossesion`, `total_shots`, `ShotsOnTarget`, and
`corners`; the four keys share the `stats All` coverage above. The only stats
period is `All`, so all `fh_` and `sh_` columns are null. Shotmaps are empty,
referee text is empty, and the stats feed has no `yellow_cards` rows. Goals
and first-half goals therefore come from the event feed, and event-unified
yellows are the only cards target.
