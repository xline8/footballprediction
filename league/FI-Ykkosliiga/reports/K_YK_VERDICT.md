# Verdict — K-YK (FI-Ykkosliiga cards) — **NOT BUILDABLE**

No preregistration was written and nothing was fitted. The only cards source
is the event feed (`Card` events), and its coverage breaks across eras:
matches with at least one Card event are 27/162 (2021), 23/164 (2022),
19/164 (2023), 26/135 (2024), 22/135 (2025) — 11.6–19.3% of matches — versus
134/135 (99.3%, 494 Card events) in 2026. The target is therefore near-complete
only in the virgin window; no dev window exists that can support the
season-blocked walk-forward protocol, and the stats feed carries no card keys
in any season (non-null stats 0/895) to fill the gap. A cards model fitted on
2026 alone would have no dev gate and no virgin left to test it on. K-YK is
NOT BUILDABLE.
