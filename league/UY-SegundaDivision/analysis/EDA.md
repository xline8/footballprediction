# Exploratory data analysis

All fitting-relevant values below are generated in [eda_summary.json](eda_summary.json) from finished 2021–2025 data. 2026 is shown only in inventory and levels tables; it does not select lines, arms, constants, or windows.

## Findings

**F1 — levels and drift.** Finished goals/match by season are 2021 2.106, 2022 2.160, 2023 2.411, 2024 2.124, 2025 2.165; 2026 inventory level is 2.326. Pooled dev evaluation seasons 2022–2025 (n=863, `pooled_eval`) have home/draw/away rates 0.394/0.275/0.331. OU2.5 over-rates are 0.376, 0.373, 0.416, 0.339, and 0.365 for 2021–2025.

**F2 — trait reliability.** Split-half reliability is chronological odd/even matches pooled by team over 2021–2025: goals-for r=0.187 (n=30 teams), goals-against r=0.617 (n=30). SOT, corners, shots, and yellows are null with n=0. Consecutive season correlations for goals-for are -0.637, 0.258, 0.577, -0.063; goals-against are 0.409, 0.443, 0.443, -0.194 (2021–22 through 2024–25).

**F3 — halves.** Event-time rule is minute `<=45`; no added-time field is available. Dev event goals give a pooled 1H share of 0.433 across 1,915 goals and 792 matches; per-season shares are 2022 0.518, 2023 0.418, 2024 0.380, 2025 0.440 — the 2022 value is ~3 SE above the pooled share, so the frozen constant carries real season-to-season wobble and is presentation-only. Per-match share variance is 0.1329. The naive career team-share split-half is r=0.330 (n=26), but it is inflated by the season mix (teams present in high-share seasons inherit it): the de-confounded checks are r=0.146 within team-season (n=54 team-seasons) and r=0.265 season-demeaned (n=26), neither significant — team 1H share is noise, and the constant must not become a fitted covariate. No SOT/corners/shots/card constant exists.

**F4 — movers (X2).** The 12 AUF relegated entrants in 2022–2025 are compared with the same-season finished Segunda team-match mean. Mean GF difference is -0.017 (7 negative, 5 positive); mean GA difference is +0.040 (5 negative, 7 positive). This is not the expected clean opposite-sign pattern; it is weak/mixed. Tier-3 entrant counts are 2, 4, 2, and 2 for 2022–2025. Cumulative pre-target frozen log offsets are in `movers_dev.frozen_log_offsets_by_target`: 2022 (0,0), 2023 (-0.124,+0.150), 2024 (-0.041,+0.114), 2025 (+0.004,+0.058), attack then defence.

**F5 — coverage.** Stats, shotmap, splits, lineups, and named referees are 0 in every season. Events cover 156/169, 214/231, 212/233, 210/230, and 202/218 payloads in 2022–2026; 2021 has none. The event feed has 318 Red and 60 YellowRed cards in dev, zero plain Yellow; these rare/incomplete event counts are not a card target.

**F6 — identity.** ID-keying merges the two display-name variant pairs recorded in inventory; no name-based team split is permitted.

**F7 — calendar.** Calendar-year labels are retained; 2023 has January 2024 spillover. Phase labels are not stable enough in payload metadata to define exclusions.

**F8 — transitional virgin.** 2026 is reserved as a transitional virgin read: 218 matches, 14 teams, last match 2026-11-01, and 12 fewer matches than 2025. It is not read for fitting-relevant decisions here.

**F9 — target feasibility.** Goals are the only observed consistent target. SOT, corners, total shots, and yellows cannot be measured.

**F10 — event completeness.** Empty event payloads occur in 2021 and some later matches; score-derived FT goals remain usable, while event-derived half/card fields are conditional on events.

**F11 — line selection.** On pooled 2022–2025 dev (`n=863`), OU1.5/2.5/3.5 over-rates are 0.648/0.373/0.173. OU2.5 is closest to 50% and is the primary line.

**F12 — home advantage.** Dev pooled rates are recorded in F1; home advantage is left to the fitted DC parameter.

**F13 — 2026 restriction.** Any 2026 number in inventory/levels is descriptive only; no 2026 row informed F2–F4, F11, or design choices.

Charts are generated by `make_charts.py` from this JSON and the built CSV.
