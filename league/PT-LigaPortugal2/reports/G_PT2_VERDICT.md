## Gate arithmetic

The dev gate requires pooled 1X2 LL and RPS to beat climatology and pooled OU2.5 LL to beat the base-rate baseline, per arm.

- Gate Zero, 1X2 LL: **1.107690 vs climatology 1.090692 — FAIL** (model does not beat climatology).
- Gate Zero, RPS: **0.234253 vs climatology 0.228875 — FAIL** (model does not beat climatology).
- Gate Zero, OU2.5 LL: **0.702741 vs base rate 0.691790 — FAIL** (model does not beat the baseline).
- SHRINK has the same three comparisons and therefore also **FAILS**.

Gate Zero failed Gate Zero's dev gate. Under the frozen rule, the virgin was not read. Virgin legs (i) 1X2 LL, (ii) home-win and OU2.5 calibration, and (iii) the variant sign check are therefore **not assessed**; calibration breaches and SEs are **N/A**. The both-pass/tie rule is not reached because no arm passed dev. **Final verdict: FAIL; nothing ships.**

## Verification notes

- `results_dev.json` and `results_dev.csv` were generated by `engine/goals/run_g_pt2.py dev`.
- Independent verification by the builder (not Codex): the 2022/2023 climatology LL was recomputed by hand from prior-season rates and matches the engine exactly (1.0925722); the 2022/2023 Gate Zero fit was replayed through the BG-SecondLeague reference DC code and reproduced the engine's LL to within 0.0002 (1.12129 vs 1.12114, a cutoff-date rounding difference) — the dev failure is the model's, not the harness's. This mirrors G-US (MLS), the program's other case of climatology beating DC outright on 1X2.
- The dev loader allows only 2021/2022–2024/2025 and asserts that 2026/2027 is never read.
- The gated virgin command reran the dev gate and wrote the not-read marker without loading the virgin season; no prediction file exists.
- The engine uses the preregistered 14 sweeps, damping 0.7, +0.25 smoothing, home advantage initialization 0.28, `MAXG=10`, rho grid, 270-day decay, and the exact SHRINK n/6 rule.
