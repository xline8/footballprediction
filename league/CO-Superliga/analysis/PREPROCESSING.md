# Step 2 — Preprocessing: CO-Superliga

## Inclusion rules

- Played matches only: all 12 index rows are finished matches
  (`header.status.finished: True`, `reason.long: Full-Time` except the
  2025 second leg, `Pen 3–4`); none excluded, none missing.
- Season labels are the warehouse `season` values `2021`…`2026`
  (calendar-year labels; the tie is played in Jan/Feb except 2021, Oct).
- No deduplication was needed: 12 index rows = 12 distinct match ids =
  12 payload files on disk.

## Parsing rules (from payload inspection, 12/12 read)

- Score: `header.teams[0/1].score`, cross-checked against goal events in
  `content.matchFacts.events.events` (29 = 29; exact match).
- Team stats (where present): `content.stats.Periods.{All,FirstHalf,
  SecondHalf}.stats[]` groups, keyed by `stat.key` (`total_shots`,
  `corners`, `ShotsOnTarget`, `yellow_cards`, `red_cards`, `fouls`,
  `expected_goals`, …). `content.stats` is `None` in 5/12 payloads
  (2021 ×2, 2022 ×2, 2024 leg 2) — recorded as missing, never imputed.
- Shotmap: `content.shotmap.shots` (list; empty `[]` in 8/12). SOT from
  the shotmap is `isOnTarget == True`; the stats-block `ShotsOnTarget`
  is the primary SOT source where stats exist (7 matches).
- Cards: only source is Card events in
  `content.matchFacts.events.events` (97 Card events in 12 matches).
  There is **no referee field** anywhere in the payloads
  (`matchFacts` has no referee key in any of the 12). The stats-block
  `yellow_cards` is zero-filled [0, 0] in the three 2023/2024 stats
  matches and is therefore NOT used as a target for those matches.
- Tie context: `content.matchFacts.infoBox.legInfo` (1st/2nd leg),
  `header.status.aggregatedStr`, `whoLostOnAggregated`,
  `whoLostOnPenalties`. The second leg's 90-minute score is the modelled
  outcome for any goals market; the aggregate/penalty outcome is a
  tie-level result, not a match-level one, and is recorded only as
  context (no model is built).

## Split-source rules

- Period splits (FirstHalf/SecondHalf) exist exactly where `content.stats`
  exists (7/12). They are used only for the descriptive 1H shares in
  EDA; no halves constants are frozen (n too small — see EDA).
- No fabrication anywhere: absent stats/shotmap stay absent in the CSV
  (empty fields), and the builder script emits them as such.

## Output

`league/CO-Superliga/data/co_superliga_matches.csv` — one row per played
match (12 rows), regenerated by `data/build_data.py` from the warehouse
index + payloads. The CSV is gitignored (repo norm); the builder is
committed. The CSV exists only as a record of the 12 matches and their
payload-verified targets; it feeds no fitting, because no fitting is done.
